// About Pragmatic Fox

Pragmatic Fox was founded on a simple idea: efficient, dependable workflows help creative teams do better work. Small improvements can remove repetition, reduce errors, and give people more time to focus on the story. We build practical tools that fit naturally into the way production teams already work—clear, useful, and made to keep demanding projects moving.

Andrew Cohen
Founder

As a VFX Editor on productions including Avatar: The Last Airbender, Gen V, and Obi-Wan Kenobi, Andrew has worked in fast-paced environments where organization, accuracy, and clear communication are essential. That experience gives him a firsthand understanding of the challenges post-production teams face, from managing large volumes of information to coordinating complex processes under tight deadlines.

Pragmatic Fox gives Andrew a place to turn that experience into tools for the film industry. Each product focuses on real-world use: reducing repetitive work, improving reliability, and helping teams spend less time fighting their workflows and more time doing their best work.
